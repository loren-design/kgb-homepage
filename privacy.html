Privacy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

The short version: we don't want your personal information. This site exists to point you toward God, not to collect data about you.

What we don't collect

No accounts. No names. No email addresses. No cookies for tracking or advertising. We never sell, rent, or share anything about you, because there's nothing to share.

What we do count

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-respecting analytics tool, to count page visits. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't store personal information — no names, no IP addresses kept, no location tracking. It just tells us how many people came through, so we know the doors are working.

Where the doors lead

The doors on this site take you to our other pages — pray.knowgodbetter.org and help.knowgodbetter.org. Those tools are built with the same conviction: prayer requests can be sent with no name at all, and we keep it that way on purpose.

Questions

If you have a question about any of this, reach out through Know God Better Ministries.
