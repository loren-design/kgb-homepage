Straight Answers

Questions People Ask

What is this, exactly?

You put on an inflatable suit, lie back, and it squeezes in slow, rolling waves — always in the direction your body naturally drains. Like several pairs of hands working in perfect rhythm, without a single hand ever touching you.

Is this a massage? Does someone touch me?

No — and honestly, that's why a lot of people choose this. No stranger's hands on you, no oils, no undressing, no lying face-down on a table. You stay fully dressed the whole time while the suit does the work. And here's the part hands can't match: hands get tired — pressure drifts, rhythm slips. The suit holds the same perfect, steady wave from the first minute to the last. Nothing weird, nothing uncomfortable. Just quiet time to yourself.

What do I wear?

Comfortable, fitted clothing — leggings, joggers, a t-shirt. You stay fully dressed the whole time. That's one of the things people love most about it.

Does it hurt?

No. Most people describe it as a gentle, wave-like squeeze — like a slow hug moving up your body. Firm, never forced — and you can ask us to adjust it anytime.

What does a session look like?

You come in, we help you into the suit, you lie back for 40 to 60 minutes, and you leave. No downtime. You can go straight back to your day.

Does it actually work?

Fair question — we'd ask it too. The technology is FDA-cleared and has been used for decades. What people commonly notice: legs feel lighter, less puffiness, deep relaxation. Results vary person to person, and we won't promise you anything a machine can't promise.

Is this for men too?

Absolutely. For comfort and privacy, sessions are matched with a same-gender practitioner — women with Jackie, men with Loren.

How often should I come?

One session feels good. A series is what changes things — that's why most of our clients run a 5 or 10 session package. Many people come weekly or every other week.

Anything I should do before or after?

Drink water before and after, and come with a light stomach — the session works your midsection too. That's it.

Is it safe for me?

It's safe for most people, but not everyone. Before your first session, we'll send you a short health form, and if anything needs a closer look, we may ask for a quick note from your doctor. That's not a no — that's us being careful with you.
