I've been there

“Why would I need Jesus if I'm already talking to His dad?”

That's what I asked a pastor. I wasn't raised with any religion — I wasn't mad at God, I just didn't know Him. I got sober in 1991, and in the late nineties I started going to church because I figured that's where God lived, and my little girl didn't need an AA meeting to learn about Him.

I kept being drawn back to church, and over the next six months I found a great pastor — a patient one — who helped me unpack that question. In 2000, I surrendered to Jesus and found the God I'd been looking for: the God of the Bible, the One who created me, the Father Jesus calls His own. And now mine.

That's why it's called Know God Better Ministries — helping people know God as the loving Father He is, not the one He's been misrepresented as.
