About Us

We live here. This is for our neighbors.

Alves Wellness is a husband-and-wife team, one machine we believe in, and one quiet room inside The Beauty Bar.

Our story

We're Jackie and Loren Alves. We live here, and we love our community.

Alves Wellness started with a question we couldn't shake: why should our friends and clients have to drive a couple hundred miles — and pay about twice as much — for something this simple and this good?

So we did the homework. We researched this technology for a long time before we brought it home to Yuma. What sold us wasn't hype — it was how simple it is. A gentle session that helps your body do what it already knows how to do.

We run it the way we'd want to be treated: by appointment only, so the room is ready and quiet when you arrive. And for your comfort and privacy, sessions are always matched with a same-gender practitioner — women with Jackie, men with Loren.

What we believe

Wellness isn't one piece of you — it's all of you. That conviction is the heart behind everything we do here.

Body

Tired legs, long days on your feet, hard training, healing from something. Your body carries it all — this helps it set some of that down.

Mind

Quiet time with nowhere to be. Most people nearly fall asleep. When your body lets go, your head does too.

Whole person

Spirit, soul, and body — we believe caring for people means caring for all of it. That's why we built this here at home.
