Why we did this

We're Jackie and Loren Alves. We live here, and we love our community.

The closest place doing anything like this is a couple hundred miles away — and it costs about twice what we charge. We don't think our friends and clients should have to drive that far, or pay that much, to feel better.

So we did the homework. We researched this for a long time before we brought it home. What sold us wasn't hype — it was how simple it is. Forty gentle minutes that help your body do what it already knows how to do.

Body

Tired legs, long days on your feet, hard training, healing from something. Your body carries it all — this helps it set some of that down.

Mind

Forty quiet minutes with nowhere to be. Most people nearly fall asleep. When your body lets go, your head does too.

Whole person

We believe wellness isn't one piece of you — it's all of you. That's the heart behind Alves Wellness, and it's why we built it here at home.
